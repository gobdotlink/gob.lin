# Dictation overlay

`OverlayController.begin` starts a session and returns its UUID. Pass that session ID to updates and delayed hides; stale callbacks from an earlier dictation are ignored. The panel is a fixed size for its whole life, so streaming text never resizes or moves it; inside it, the speech bubble grows one line at a time up to four lines and older lines scroll under a top fade. Errors carry their `DiagnosticFailure`, so the overlay can pick the goblin's face, hand and first-person sentence; History and Diagnostics keep the failure's own message. The settings preview is explicitly synthetic.

Audio frames drain on a separate task from UI events. Microphone levels are coalesced and rendered at most 20 times per second; they do not reorder the panel. While listening, a non-terminal warning can appear under the header when the opened device reports mute or zero input volume, even if its samples contain noise. Without a device-reported cause, digital silence warns after 5 s before any sound or 15 s after sound was heard. Device warnings persist until the hardware report clears; sample-only warnings clear when sound returns, and a valid generic silence notice can remain after hardware recovery. These warnings never end the recording. Recoverable live revisions remain in diagnostics without replacing the recording header. A terminal preview failure latches one recording notice until the next session. Stop ends preview publication and scheduling while final capture frames still reach recognition. After a paste shortcut is sent, the overlay closes unless cleanup already produced a warning; that warning stays visible for four seconds. An app that cannot verify the edit leaves the transcript on the clipboard and the previous clipboard recoverable without reporting a confirmed insertion.

The panel is non-activating and cannot become key or main, uses status-bar level, joins all Spaces, and is a full-screen auxiliary. It sits in the bottom-right corner of the pointer's screen, 16 pt inside the visible frame (above the Dock), or top-right below the menu bar for the Top setting. Right after it appears, the focused element's caret is read off the main thread with a 50 ms Accessibility timeout; if the caret falls inside the overlay's corner while it is still arming, the whole layout mirrors to the left corner before any words show. The mouth swaps between the shut and yap frames with hysteresis (open above 0.18, close below 0.12) at most 12 times a second. DESIGN.md lists each phase's face, hand and copy.

Checks: SwiftPM and Xcode builds. Actual full-screen/Spaces/multi-monitor behavior requires a Mac with those arrangements; do not claim it from a source review alone.

Showing fades in and rises 8 pt over 140 ms with ease-out; hiding takes 120 ms; Reduce Motion keeps the fade and drops the movement. The panel orders out once the exit settles. A `begin()` during an exit reverses from the current on-screen state. After a paste lands, the bubble folds into an "inserted" chip for about 0.6 s; the first dictation past 1,000, 10,000, 100,000 or a million words shows a one-time milestone instead (switchable under Settings › Overlay). Thresholds already passed when this shipped are recorded quietly rather than celebrated late.

Hiding ends the session; a later callback must call `begin()` to show a new state. Delayed hides reject stale session IDs before canceling any timer. Display changes re-resolve the stored screen number and fall back to the main screen if it is removed. Physical display removal/Spaces/fullscreen remain hardware checks.
