gob.lin · final direction · built from Artem's and Will's picks

One goblin, one system.

Whimsy's look and voice are the base: light-first with a real dark mode, moss green, and short sentences in the goblin's own voice. Each other direction contributes the piece you both picked, redrawn in that style rather than pasted in. Lair gives the corner speech bubble, Phosphor the one-line “what happened” notes, and Ambient the popover. Instrument gives the settings plates and status lights. The pixel art merges three sheets into one cast.

Whimsy is budgeted by how often you see it.

The overlay shows up a hundred times a day, so it gets a glance and a moving mouth, nothing more. First run, empty states, milestones and About carry the jokes.

One rendering language per layer.

Controls are stock SF shapes. Pixel art is only the goblin, its hands and its props, at whole-number scales, and never inside a control row.

Status is a light plus a word.

Instrument's green, amber, red and off lights mark state in the popover, settings and setup. A word always sits next to the light, so colour is never the only signal.

Say what happened, once.

Every processing state and error has one plain subline, Phosphor's idea: which model, how long, what failed, and what was kept. It's set in SF Pro, without the terminal styling.

  • Bottom-right, 16 pt in from the edge and 16 pt above the Dock, or above the screen edge when the Dock is hidden or on the side.
  • It mirrors before it appears. If the focused caret is inside that corner, everything opens bottom-left instead. It never moves while you talk.

What's in it

  • Whimsy's panel, unchanged inside: a 5-bar meter, the label, the timer and ×. Words are SF Pro 15/21, and tentative words drop to 42% white.
  • Always dark glass at 92%, so it reads over any app in either appearance. A 10 pt tail points at the goblin's mouth.
  • The goblin sits beside the bubble, not on it, at 128 pt (Lair's 2×, 4 device px per art px). The whole head shows, so the mouth sync is visible. If it tests too big, crop from the brow down rather than shrink it.

Motion

Why a popover

  • Daily things are one click deep: mic check, cleanup on or off, tone, and the last few dictations with search.
  • Typing anywhere in it focuses search, which filters an in-memory index of the last 1,000 entries on every keystroke.
  • Rare and admin commands stay a real NSMenu, behind ⋯ or a right-click, so keyboard and VoiceOver users keep a plain list.

Build and motion

  • Concentric radii: panel 22, module 14, row 8, with 8 pt and 6 pt insets.
  • Opens by scaling 0.98→1 with a fade over 160 ms, ease-out, anchored at the icon. Row actions fade in over 80 ms. Reduced motion: fade only.
