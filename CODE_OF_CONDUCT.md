# Community code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Welcome different experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, share someone else's private information, or post sexual content in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior harms other contributors. Contact the repository owner privately through GitHub to report a concern; do not publish another person's private information in an issue. Maintainers will consider context, protect the privacy of reports where possible, and apply proportionate measures.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents this project.
